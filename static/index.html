How to Stream Videos to YouTube Live

  1. 1. Open YouTube Studio: Go to studio.youtube.com and click "Go Live" (top-right).
  2. 2. Copy your Stream Key: Under "Stream Settings", copy your Default Stream Key (e.g. xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx).
  3. 3. Paste into SkyOBS Studio: Paste your Stream Key into the "YouTube Stream Key" box on the right.
  4. 4. Add Videos from your PC: Click "Add Videos" or "Add Folder" to select your local video files.
  5. 5. Set Loop Mode: Choose "Loop All" for a continuous 24/7 stream, or "Loop One" to repeat a single video.
  6. 6. Click "START STREAMING": SkyOBS Studio will stream your videos directly to YouTube in realtime with zero frame drops!
